# LinkedIn — Organic Founder-Story Post

**Where to post:** Your personal LinkedIn profile → https://www.linkedin.com/feed/
**Best time:** Tue–Thu, 8–10am EST or 5–7pm EST
**Frequency:** Once. If high engagement, do a follow-up "thanks + what's next" post in 5–7 days.

---

## Pre-post profile checklist (do this FIRST)

A great post on a bare profile converts at zero. Spend 10 min on this before posting anything:

- [ ] **Headline** — change to: `FAU MSBA candidate · Building OffPitchOS — software for youth soccer clubs`
- [ ] **About section** — first 2 lines must mention OffPitchOS. Example opener:
      > Finishing my MSBA at Florida Atlantic University this summer. I built OffPitchOS — an operating system for youth soccer clubs in South Florida. We're locking in our first 10 clubs at 50% off forever.
- [ ] **Featured section** — pin one of:
      - the demo video link (https://youtu.be/ZylUkBkgqIs), OR
      - a clean screenshot of the dashboard
- [ ] **Profile photo** — clean head-and-shoulders, neutral background, no sunglasses, no kids-on-sideline cropped from a team photo
- [ ] **Banner image** — optional but nice: a clean OffPitchOS-branded banner or a wide soccer field photo

---

## Post content (copy-paste)

```
I'm finishing my MSBA at Florida Atlantic this summer, and instead of writing a thesis I shipped a software platform for youth soccer clubs.

Spent the last year watching South Florida clubs run on spreadsheets, GroupMe threads, and panic texts. The pattern was always the same: a U14 coach drops at 6pm Friday → DOC has 14 hours to find a sub and tell 22 parents → it falls on the Director's personal phone.

So I built OffPitchOS. Voice command → "cancel U14 practice tonight, weather" → schedule updates, parents notified, sub coach pinged. One sentence handles what used to take 90 minutes.

90-second demo: https://youtu.be/ZylUkBkgqIs

If you know a Director of Coaching at a South Florida youth club, I'd love a 15-minute intro. First 10 clubs lock in at 50% off forever.

Reply or DM — I'm building this with real clubs, not building it alone.
```

---

## Optional line — only if it's actually true

Just before the closing line, add:

> I'm not joining any company after graduation. I'm betting on this.

This line lands hard if true. If you're keeping job-search optionality, leave it out — Directors of Coaching can sniff a hedged founder.

---

## After posting

- Reply to every comment within 1 hour (LinkedIn algo rewards fast engagement)
- Send a connection request + a 1-line DM to anyone who reacts or comments
- DM script: "Hey [name] — appreciate the reaction. You connected to anyone running a youth club in South Florida? Even a coach-level intro helps."
- Don't repost the same content for at least 14 days
- Track every DOC intro that comes from this post in your outreach CSV

---

## Variations to try if you do a second post in 5–7 days

- **Update post:** "5 days since I posted about OffPitchOS — here's what happened: [N intros, N demos, N close conversations]"
- **Story post:** Pick one DOC conversation you had, write a 4-paragraph teardown of what they're using today vs. what one OffPitchOS feature fixed for them.
- **Pain post:** Pick one specific problem (Friday coach drops, parent payment chasing, scheduling chaos) and write the brutal version. End with: "If this is your Friday, DM me."

---

## What NOT to do

- Don't use hashtags on personal LinkedIn (they hurt reach now, not help)
- Don't tag people who haven't engaged first
- Don't post the same content to your company page AND personal page same day (LinkedIn algo deboosts dupes)
- Don't run paid promotion on this post (organic-only test first)
