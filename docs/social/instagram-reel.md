# Instagram Reel — Demo

**Where to post:** OffPitchOS Instagram account (or your personal IG if no brand account yet)
**Best time:** Tue/Wed/Thu, 11am–1pm EST (peak Reels engagement)
**Frequency:** One Reel per week max. Quality > volume.

---

## Pre-post checklist

- [ ] OffPitchOS Instagram account exists and is set to **Business** (gives you analytics + Reel insights)
- [ ] Profile bio: `FAU MSBA grad · Building software for youth soccer clubs · 🇺🇸 South Florida`
- [ ] Link in bio: https://offpitchos.com (or a Linktree with demo + Calendly + signup)
- [ ] Profile picture matches your LinkedIn profile picture (cross-platform recognition)
- [ ] At least 3 grid posts before posting Reels (a 1-post-3-Reel account looks like a bot)

---

## Video source

90-second demo: https://youtu.be/ZylUkBkgqIs

**For a Reel, trim to 30–60 seconds max** (Reels >60s get suppressed by the algo). Best cuts:
- **0:00–0:03 hook** — coach saying "Cancel U14 practice tonight, weather" with phone close-up. NO logo, NO title card on screen yet. The hook is the action.
- **0:03–0:20** — UI showing schedule updating, parents getting the push, sub coach getting pinged. Fast cuts, no voiceover.
- **0:20–0:35** — text overlay: "One sentence. Done in 4 seconds. Built for South FL youth soccer clubs."
- **0:35–0:45** — closing card with offpitchos.com + "First 10 clubs · 50% off forever"

If you want to crank it out fast: just take the YouTube demo, screen-record the most visual 45 seconds (the voice-command moment is the money shot), and post.

## Engagement tactics

- Reply to every comment in the first 60 minutes (algo signal)
- DM anyone who comments "demo" or "interested" → send the YouTube link directly
- Save the Reel to a Highlight on your profile (titled "Demo")
- Cross-post the Reel to Facebook Reels too — costs 30 seconds, doubles the surface area

---

## DO NOT

- Don't post the unedited 90-second YouTube video as a Reel. The hook needs to be in the first 1.5 seconds or the Reel dies.
- Don't add music if your demo has voice command audio — the voice IS the hook
- Don't tag random soccer accounts or DOCs without their permission
- Don't run paid promotion on this Reel until you've done 3+ organic Reels first (organic baseline matters)
- Don't cross-post the same Reel to YouTube Shorts and TikTok same day — IG algo deboosts duplicates
